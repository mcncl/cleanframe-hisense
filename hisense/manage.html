Artwork

After uploading, select “Your photo” in the TV’s Wallpaper settings. Images are cropped to fit the screen.

When Home is idle

This uses a local service; a brief glimpse of the original Home screen may appear. It leaves Art Gallery and other apps alone. Reload CleanFrame after changing the idle time.

Your apps

Install missing apps from the TV’s App Store, then refresh this list. A Fire TV shortcut switches to the stick; it does not open a particular Fire TV app.
